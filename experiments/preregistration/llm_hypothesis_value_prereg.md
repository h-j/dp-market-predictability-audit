## 1. Study Motivation & Scientific Context

Prior substrate research tracks established market predictability bounds:
1. Single-name daily/3-day direction is unpredictable ($MCC \approx 0.00$).
2. 5-day realized volatility is predictable ($R^2_{vs\_pers} = +0.26 \text{ to } +0.40$), but daily-bar features add no monetizable edge beyond India VIX (**H-V3** confirmed).
3. Option volatility regime filtering (**Gate G-OPT**) failed to beat unconditional variance risk premium harvesting.
4. Cross-sectional ranking (**Gate G-XS1/G-XS2**) yields a **CROSS-SECTIONAL NULL**.

This Phase 4 study shifts from market predictability to evaluating **LLM cognition value**:
> **Core Question**: Do trading hypotheses generated by the substrate LLM (via reflective cognition / prompt pipeline) survive out-of-sample walk-forward backtesting at a rate statistically superior to randomly generated hypotheses from the exact same structural grammar?

If LLM-generated hypotheses do not outperform random grammar sampling, substrate hypothesis generation is empirically proven to be **pseudo-reasoning (hallucinated prompt overfitting)**.

---

## 2. Structural Hypothesis Grammar Definition ($G$)

A valid hypothesis $H \in G$ consists of a tuple $\langle \text{Trigger Predicate}, \text{Target Mode}, \text{Prediction Direction} \rangle$:

1. **Feature Scope ($F$)**:
   - `rv_1d`, `rv_5d`, `vix_close`, `vix_change_5d`, `vix_percentile_252d`, `volume_ratio_5d`, `norm_gap`, `delivery_pct`, `rs_nifty_3m`.
2. **Operators ($\text{OP}$)**:
   - `GREATER_THAN` ($>$), `LESS_THAN` ($<$), `BETWEEN` ($[\text{q}_{lo}, \text{q}_{hi}]$).
3. **Thresholds ($T$)**:
   - Quantile boundaries $\{0.20, 0.35, 0.50, 0.65, 0.80\}$ computed over historical expanding training windows.
4. **Target Modes**:
   - `volatility_5d` (5-day realized volatility forecasting).
   - `vol_regime_expansion` (predicting volatility rise above persistence).
   - `direction_3d` (3-day directional change).

---

## 3. Sample Generation Protocols

- **Treatment Group ($H_{\text{LLM}}$, $N=50$)**: Structured hypotheses generated by the Substrate LLM (Ollama/Llama3 or structured prompt pipeline) given market observations, reflections, and regime context, compiled into grammar $G$.
- **Control Group ($H_{\text{Random}}$, $N=50$)**: Pseudo-hypotheses generated by uniformly sampling valid features, operators, thresholds, and targets from grammar $G$ using a fixed seed (`seed=42`).

---

## 4. Backtesting & Evaluation Protocol

- **Backtest Engine**: Expanding-window walk-forward validation (initial train size=250, test fold size=100, stride-5 non-overlapping evaluation) across `NIFTY`, `RELIANCE`, and `TCS`.
- **Out-of-Sample Metrics**:
  - Out-of-sample $R^2_{\text{vs\_pers}}$ (for volatility targets).
  - QLIKE loss reduction.
  - Binary classification MCC & directional accuracy.
- **Definition of Hypothesis Survival**:
  A hypothesis $H_i$ **survives** if its out-of-sample $R^2_{\text{vs\_pers}} > 0.05$ **AND** its binary classification MCC $> 0.10$ out-of-sample ($p < 0.05$).

## 5. Statistical Hypothesis Tests & Pre-Registered Gates

> [!IMPORTANT]
> - **G-LLM1 (Hypothesis Survival Advantage)**: Out-of-sample survival rate (% of surviving hypotheses) for $H_{\text{LLM}}$ is statistically significantly greater than $H_{\text{Random}}$ at $p < 0.05$ via Fisher's Exact Test / Permutation Test:
>   $$p_{\text{Fisher}} < 0.05 \quad \text{AND} \quad \text{SurvivalRate}(H_{\text{LLM}}) > \text{SurvivalRate}(H_{\text{Random}})$$
