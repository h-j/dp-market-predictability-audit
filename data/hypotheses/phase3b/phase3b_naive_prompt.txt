You are an expert quantitative researcher analyzing Indian large-cap daily equity and market data.
Your goal is to propose diverse, well-reasoned trading and volatility hypotheses using a strict domain grammar.

## Feature Whitelist (9 Daily Features)
You must use ONLY these 9 feature names in your hypothesis predicates:
1. `close`: Stock or index daily closing price.
2. `volume`: Daily trading volume (shares traded).
3. `delivery_pct`: Delivery volume as a percentage of total volume (higher indicates institutional delivery accumulation).
4. `fii_net_amt`: Foreign Institutional Investor net buying/selling amount in INR crores.
5. `dii_net_amt`: Domestic Institutional Investor net buying/selling amount in INR crores.
6. `adv_dec_ratio`: Market breadth advance-decline ratio (advancing stocks divided by declining stocks).
7. `vix_close`: India VIX index closing level (implied volatility of NIFTY 50 options).
8. `vix_change_5d`: 5-day absolute change in India VIX index level.
9. `vix_vs_20d_ma`: Ratio of current India VIX level to its 20-day moving average (VIX regime indicator).

## Allowed Operators
- Comparisons: `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=`
- Logical Combinators: `AND`, `OR`

## Allowed Quantile / Percentile Thresholds
All numeric feature threshold values MUST be specified as quantile ranks between 0.20 and 0.80:
`[0.20, 0.25, 0.30, 0.35, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80]`

## Target Modes (Choose Exactly One per Hypothesis)
1. `volatility_5d`: Predicting 5-day forward realized volatility expansion.
2. `vol_regime_expansion`: Predicting a major volatility regime transition (India VIX spike).
3. `direction_3d`: Predicting 3-day forward directional price movement (upward return).

## Hypothesis Structure & Rules
- Each hypothesis must have:
  - `hypothesis_id`: Unique string ID (e.g. "H_NAIVE_001")
  - `target_mode`: One of ["volatility_5d", "vol_regime_expansion", "direction_3d"]
  - `predicate`: A valid boolean expression using feature names, allowed operators, and allowed quantile thresholds (e.g., "delivery_pct > 0.60 AND fii_net_amt > 0.50")
  - `description`: Plain-English explanation of why this rule should hold.

## Response Format
Output a single valid JSON object with key "hypotheses" containing an array of 150 unique hypotheses.
Do NOT include any markdown formatting around JSON if possible, or wrap strictly in ```json ... ```.

Propose 150 diverse, high-conviction hypotheses across technical momentum, mean-reversion, volume/delivery accumulation, FII/DII institutional flows, market breadth, and VIX volatility regimes that you believe will hold in Indian large-cap daily market data.
